# Clarification and Query Register — ProcureSphere 360

PRD Reference: HPE-PRD-2026-PROC01 / VER-1.0-DRAFT  
Development Partner: VPD Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

This document logs all minimal, PRD-faithful interpretations, assumptions, and query clarifications made during implementation.

---

| ID | Requirement Area | Question / Ambiguity | Business / Engineering Impact | Proposed Minimal PRD-Faithful Assumption | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ASSUMP-001 | Approval Policy Routing | How should delegates be resolved if an approver in a multi-level chain is unavailable? | Approval process could stall without delegate fallback. | If an approver has an active delegate (`is_active=True`, within effective start/end dates), the approval task and permission auto-delegate to that assigned user while retaining audit records of both target approver and delegate actor. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-002 | Budget Reservation Lifecycle | At what exact state transition does PR budget reservation move to PO commitment and Invoice actual spend? | Financial double-counting or unreserved spend risk. | Requisition approval reserves funds (`BudgetReservation` state=RESERVED). PO issuance converts reservation to commitment (`SpendLedger` type=COMMITMENT). Supplier invoice approval converts commitment to actual spend (`SpendLedger` type=ACTUAL). | Documented |
| ASSUMP-003 | Sealed Bidding Stage Control | When are sealed bids made readable by evaluators? | Sealed bid confidentiality compliance. | Bids remain strictly unreadable by reviewers/evaluators until `SourcingEvent.status` transitions out of `BID_WINDOW` (e.g. to `TECHNICAL_REVIEW`). Vendors can edit/withdraw bids only during `BID_WINDOW`. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-004 | 3-Way Match Tolerances | Should tolerances be evaluated per line item or overall invoice total? | Payment variance handling & match exceptions. | Tolerances support both percentage and flat amount thresholds configured per organization/category. Exceptions (`MatchException`) are raised per line item variance as well as summary variance if exceeded. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-005 | Contract Renewal Alert Schedule | How often should Celery Beat execute contract alert scans? | Notification volume vs. timely renewal notice. | Celery Beat triggers a daily midnight scan for contracts reaching 90, 60, 30 days before expiration, or approaching milestone deadlines. Alerts avoid duplication via unique alert execution tracking. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-006 | Document Numbering Integrity | How to ensure sequential document numbers (PR-, RFQ-, PO-, GRN-, INV-, CON-) across concurrent sessions? | Gaps or duplicate numbers under concurrency. | Document numbers generated inside PostgreSQL atomic transactions using `select_for_update` on sequence counters or atomic prefix formatting. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-007 | Authentication Strategy | Transition from dual JWT/Session authentication to pure Session Cookie authentication. | API and web portal authentication security model. | Per project lead directive, SimpleJWT token authentication is removed completely. Authentication is standardized exclusively on secure HTTP-Only session cookies (`sessionid`) with CSRF protection (`csrftoken`), login throttling via `django-axes`, and dedicated `/api/v1/auth/login/` / `/logout/` endpoints. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-008 | Sourcing — Vendor Eligibility | Which vendors may be invited to an RFQ/RFP? | Ineligible vendors receiving invitations / bidding. | Only `ACTIVE` vendors (KYC approved, not on hold, not suspended) are eligible. Re-checked at publish time; a vendor that became ineligible after invitation blocks publish until the invitation is removed. Risk level does **not** block invitation (no PRD rule) — it is displayed to the executive. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-009 | Vendor suspension with open transactions (PRD 4.2) | What happens to open invitations, bids, pending awards and POs when a vendor is suspended? | Contract/PO continuity vs. compliance. | **OPEN DECISION.** Current behaviour: nothing is auto-cancelled. The open exposure is shown before the change and recorded in the audit `new_state.open_transactions_at_change`. A suspended vendor is blocked from new invitations, bid save/submit, award approval and PO issuance. Existing issued POs continue. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-010 | Sourcing — Bid closure | May procurement close a bid window before the published deadline? | Fairness to vendors still preparing bids. | **OPEN DECISION.** Not supported: closure happens only after `bid_end_date` (server clock), via Celery Beat (`sync_sourcing_bid_windows_task`, every 5 min), on-access sync, or the manual "Close bidding" action. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-011 | Sourcing — Clarification visibility | Are clarification answers shared with all invited vendors or only the asker? | Information parity between bidders. | **OPEN DECISION.** Existing behaviour retained: Q&A is visible only to the asking vendor and to internal users. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-012 | Supplier scorecard weighting | How do Responsiveness and SLA (PRD 3.3) weigh into the composite score? | Ranking of suppliers. | **OPEN DECISION.** All six indicators are computed from transactions and stored (NULL when no data — never fabricated). The composite keeps the existing approved weights (Delivery 30 / Quality 30 / Price 20 / Compliance 20), re-normalised over indicators that have data. Responsiveness and SLA are reported but not weighted until the business confirms weights. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-013 | Sourcing — Mandatory sections | When are the technical / commercial sections "required" for publish? | Publishing incomplete events. | **OPEN DECISION.** PRD does not define it, so they are optional. Publish requires title, type, description, a valid future bid window, weights totalling 100 and at least one eligible invited vendor. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-014 | Sourcing — Two-envelope visibility | When may evaluators see prices? | Sealed-bid integrity (PRD 4.1). | Bids are invisible to evaluators until the bid window closes. During TECHNICAL REVIEW only the technical envelope (technical response, technical/compliance attachments) is visible. Pricing, lines, commercial proposal and commercial attachments unseal at COMMERCIAL REVIEW. Enforced in selectors, serializers and the attachment download view. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-015 | Role split — Executive vs Manager | Which governance and award actions belong to which role? | Segregation of duties. | Per PRD 3.2 and the RBAC matrix: PROC_EXEC records risk assessments, places/releases HOLD, reviews/approves KYC, runs sourcing, recommends awards and generates POs. PROC_MGR additionally suspends/reinstates vendors and approves/rejects award recommendations. **OPEN DECISION:** a manager may still award directly (`evaluate_and_award_sourcing_event_service`); recommender ≠ approver is not enforced. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-016 | Vendor lifecycle — APPROVED state | PRD lists APPROVED before ACTIVE. Is APPROVED a resting state? | Lifecycle reporting. | **OPEN DECISION.** Existing `approve_vendor_service` moves SUBMITTED/KYC_REVIEW straight to ACTIVE. Retained unchanged. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-017 | ON_HOLD vendors and POs | May an on-hold vendor be issued a PO or awarded? | Commercial continuity. | Award approval requires an ACTIVE vendor. PO issuance blocks only SUSPENDED vendors (PRD rule 9). **OPEN DECISION** whether HOLD should also block POs. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-018 | Negotiation | PRD 3.3 lists "negotiation notes" under Evaluation & Award; the sourcing lifecycle has no NEGOTIATION state. | Workflow shape. | Negotiation is captured as append-only `NegotiationNote` records per bid during COMMERCIAL REVIEW / AWARD APPROVAL. No extra state, and no post-close price revision by vendors. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-019 | Withdrawn bids | Can a vendor re-submit after withdrawing? | Bid integrity. | **OPEN DECISION.** Not currently supported. Withdrawal snapshots the bid into `BidVersion` and marks it WITHDRAWN; it cannot be amended or re-submitted. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-020 | Vendor performance issue threshold | What composite score flags "performance issues"? | Governance attention list. | Composite < 70 (matches the existing scorecard colour bands: ≥85 good, 70–84.99 watch, <70 issue). | Documented |
| ASSUMP-021 | Procurement Executive Dashboard — RBAC scope | Task brief says "only PROC_EXEC". Should SUPER_ADMIN / PROC_MGR be locked out of the new HTMX endpoints? | Lockout of admins; inconsistency with `docs/rbac_matrix.md`. | Minimal interpretation: new HTMX views enforce the **existing permission helpers** (`can_operate_governance`, `can_manage_events`, `can_read_bids`, PO-execute = PROC_EXEC/PROC_MGR/SUPER_ADMIN). PROC_EXEC is the primary role; SUPER_ADMIN and PROC_MGR retain access exactly as on the equivalent full-page views. No new role or permission object introduced. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-022 | Procurement Executive Dashboard — placement | Where does the 4-pillar HTMX workspace live? | Scope discipline (no invented modules). | No new Django app. Fragments live in their owning apps (`vendors`, `sourcing`, `orders` views/urls/templates); the shell is the existing role home `pages/dashboards/procurement_dashboard.html` extended with 4 HTMX tabs. PO amendment is the only previously-missing wiring (`POAmendForm` + `orders_amend_modal` view calling `amend_purchase_order_service`). | Documented |
| ASSUMP-023 | Notification feed — `action_url` vs `target_url` | Brief asks for an `action_url` field; the shipped `Notification` model already persists `target_url` (migrations 0001–0003, API serializers, full-page views). | Renaming the column would break applied migrations and the API contract. | Keep the `target_url` column; expose an `action_url` property alias (getter + setter) on the model so both names work. Added composite indexes `(recipient, is_read, -created_at)` + `(recipient, -created_at)` and `Meta.ordering = ["-created_at"]` (migration 0004). | Documented |
| ASSUMP-024 | Notification widgets — dashboard placement | Brief targets only `proc_exec.html`, but no view renders that file; the live PROC_EXEC home is `procurement_dashboard.html` (see `apps/core/views.py::home_view`). | Implementing only in the orphan file would leave the feature invisible. | Implemented the HTMX widgets in **both**: `proc_exec.html` rewritten per the brief (polling + mark-read), and the same two widgets added to the live `procurement_dashboard.html` below the 4-pillar workspace. Fragments/partials live in the owning `notifications` app. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-025 | Notification mark-read — audit | AGENTS.md requires audited state changes; marking a notification read is a per-user inbox flag, not a business workflow transition. | Spamming `AuditLog` with one row per read would bury real evidence. | Mark-read persists `is_read` transactionally (`select_for_update`) but writes **no** `AuditLog`/`ApprovalAction` — consistent with the existing `notification_open_view` behaviour. Cross-user ids return 404 (no ID enumeration). | Documented |
| ASSUMP-026 | Vendor KYC — who verifies documents vs who activates | The PRD/RBAC matrix gives KYC review & approval to PROC_EXEC/PROC_MGR and does not name LEGAL_MGR for vendors. The requested workflow has Legal reviewing legal/regulatory documents. | Segregation of duties; a Legal reviewer with no activation power. | LEGAL_MGR is added as a **KYC document reviewer** (verify / reject with reason / request corrections) but **cannot start review or activate**. PROC_EXEC/PROC_MGR/SUPER_ADMIN keep review rights (unchanged from the RBAC matrix) and are the only activators. Activation is blocked until every current document is VERIFIED. **OPEN DECISION:** if strict segregation is wanted, remove PROC_EXEC from `KYC_REVIEW_ROLES` in `vendors/permissions.py` so only Legal/Manager can verify. | Open Decision |
| ASSUMP-027 | Vendor KYC — mandatory document types | The PRD does not list mandatory KYC documents. | Activation without required evidence. | Minimum rule: at least one uploaded document, all current documents verified, none rejected/pending. Specific mandatory types are configuration (`VENDOR_MANDATORY_KYC_DOC_TYPES`, default none). A replacement upload supersedes an older document of the same type for gating; the old one stays on file. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-028 | Vendor KYC — "other compliance checks" | The PRD defines no financial/other KYC check object. | Status panel shows Other checks. | Displayed as "Not required"; no check is invented. Add a check model only when the PRD defines one. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-029 | Document Numbering Integrity (VMR-CR-010) | How to avoid primary key/unique number collisions when rows are deleted? | Crashes on vendor/PR/PO/GRN creation if count()+1 matches existing records. | Implemented `generate_document_number` helper querying the highest existing sequence suffix + 1 with concurrency while-check, guaranteeing unique IDs across deletions. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-030 | Requisition Deletion & Audit Trail (PR-02, SMK-PR-038) | Can purchase requisitions be hard deleted from the database? | Audit trail loss and referential integrity disruption. | Direct hard deletion is forbidden (`ValidationError`). Requisitions in DRAFT status can be soft-cancelled to `CANCELLED`, preserving all line items, timestamps, and full `AuditLog` history. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-031 | Approval Policy Fail-Closed Governance (PR-08, SMK-PR-050) | What happens if a submitted transaction lacks a matching approval policy? | Unapproved spend or policy bypass. | Submissions and approvals fail-closed (`ValidationError`). Submitting an approval action validates that the acting user holds the configured role for the current step in the active approval policy. | Documented |
| ASSUMP-032 | Cumulative 3-Way Match Quantity Variance (INV-CR-016) | How should quantity variance be measured across partial deliveries and multiple supplier invoices? | Potential over-billing if each invoice checks only line totals. | Invoice line quantity is matched against remaining accepted quantity (total accepted across all GRNs for that PO line minus previously invoiced quantities on active/matched invoices). | Documented |
| ASSUMP-033 | SLA Approval Reminders & Escalation (A-6) | What intervals trigger reminders vs management escalations? | Stalled approvals impacting sourcing and fulfillment cycles. | Celery Beat periodic job executes hourly: PRs and exceptions pending > 48h emit reminders to step approvers / Finance; pending > 72h trigger management escalation notifications to Procurement Managers and Super Admins with immutable `AuditLog` evidence. | Documented |
