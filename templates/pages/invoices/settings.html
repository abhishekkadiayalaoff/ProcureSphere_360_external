How it works

The 3-way match process automatically verifies the Invoice against the Purchase Order and the Goods Receipt.

  • If the invoice unit price exceeds the PO unit price by more than the Price Variance Tolerance, an exception is flagged.
  • If the invoiced quantity exceeds the received quantity by more than the Quantity Variance Tolerance, an exception is flagged.
